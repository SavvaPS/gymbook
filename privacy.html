Privacy policy

The short version: GymSmith keeps your workouts on your phone. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, see, sell or share your workout or bodyweight data.

Who we are

GymSmith is a workout-tracking app made by Savva Spanos, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. In this policy, "we" and "us" means the developer of GymSmith. For UK data protection law, we are responsible for the app's privacy practices. You can contact us at gymsmith.app@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. This includes:

  • workouts, sets, reps, weights, times and notes
  • routines, programmes, skill progress and lift goals
  • bodyweight entries, if you choose to log them
  • your settings, such as equipment, rest timer and theme

In the iPhone and Android apps, a copy of this data is also kept in the app's own private storage folder on your device, so it survives if the phone clears temporary storage. Like other app data, it may be included in your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) if you have that turned on. That backup is managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

Bodyweight and training history can be personal, so we have designed the app so that this information never leaves your device unless you choose to move it yourself (see backups and exports below).

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or password is needed to use the app.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools are built into the app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health-app data is accessed.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Rest timer alerts

In the iPhone and Android apps, GymSmith can show a "Rest over" alert when your rest timer ends while the app is in the background or your phone is locked. These alerts are scheduled on your device by the app itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and the app asks your permission before showing any. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's notification settings.

Backups and exports

You can create a backup file or export your history as a spreadsheet (CSV). These files are created on your device and saved wherever you choose, for example your Files app, a cloud drive or an email to yourself. Once you save or send a file somewhere, that service's own privacy policy applies to it. We never receive these files.

Purchases

If GymSmith offers an optional paid upgrade, the payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. The store tells the app only whether the upgrade has been bought, so that it can be unlocked.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • in the app, go to Settings → Clear data to delete your history or everything; or
  • delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data. Keep a backup if you want one.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) and similar laws elsewhere, you have rights to access, correct, delete and move your personal data, and to object to how it is used. As all of your GymSmith data is held on your device and under your control, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

GymSmith is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding an optional cloud sync, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Health and safety disclaimer

GymSmith is not medical advice. It is a training log and general fitness guide. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified coach.

  • Check with a professional first if you are new to exercise, pregnant or recently gave birth, returning from injury or surgery, or have a heart, blood-pressure, joint, back or other medical condition.
  • Stop and get help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp or worsening pain.
  • Train within your ability. Exercise guides, programmes, skill progressions, weight suggestions and hints are general information. They do not know your health, history or form. Use a weight and range of movement you can control, warm up, and progress gradually.
  • Use equipment safely. Use collars, safety bars or a spotter for heavy lifts, check that bars, rings, bands and anchors are secure, and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Estimates are estimates. Figures such as estimated one-rep max, strength compared with bodyweight and muscle balance tips are approximate and should not be used to judge what is safe for you.
  • Third-party content. Linked videos and articles are produced by others. We do not control them and are not responsible for their accuracy.

You use GymSmith and take part in any exercise at your own risk.
